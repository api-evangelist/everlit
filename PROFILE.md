# Audio, Video, Social & Podcasts for the Written Word

Everlit is a B2B SaaS distribution and engagement platform that converts written content (articles, blog posts, newsletters) into audio, social video, and podcasts, then auto-distributes it. Capabilities include AI text-to-audio with voice cloning across 70+ languages, embeddable audio players, podcast syndication (Apple, Spotify, RSS/Podcast 2.0), social video generation, programmatic ad monetization, analytics, and CMS integrations (WordPress plugin plus a JavaScript embed).
